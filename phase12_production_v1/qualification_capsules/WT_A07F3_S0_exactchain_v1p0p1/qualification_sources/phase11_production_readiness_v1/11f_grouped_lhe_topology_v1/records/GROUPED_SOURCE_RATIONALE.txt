PHASE-11F GROUPED EXTERNAL-STATE SOURCE
======================================

Physics final state:
unchanged.

Old ordering:
b, bbar, e+, nue, mu-, numubar

New ordering:
e+, nue, b, mu-, numubar, bbar

Reason:
The old interleaved ordering produces non-contiguous top daughter sets in the
WHIZARD LHE resonance history. PYTHIA/LHA daughter relationships are encoded as
inclusive first/last index ranges and therefore require contiguous children.

The grouped ordering follows the FCC-ee full-six-fermion ttbar convention:

3+4       ~ W+
3+4+5     ~ t

6+7       ~ W-
6+7+8     ~ tbar

The resonance-history settings remain unchanged.

This is a serialization/topology correction, not an intended physics-model
change.

The old Phase-11 integration grids are not assumed reusable because the
external-leg ordering changes the process/phase-space representation.
