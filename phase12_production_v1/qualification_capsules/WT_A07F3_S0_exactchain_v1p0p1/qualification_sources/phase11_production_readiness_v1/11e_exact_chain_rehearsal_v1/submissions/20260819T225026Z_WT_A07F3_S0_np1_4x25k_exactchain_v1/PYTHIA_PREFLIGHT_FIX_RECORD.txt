PHASE-11E PYTHIA PREFLIGHT FIX
==============================

No PYTHIA jobs were submitted.

The initial HTCondor dry-run exposed two submit-layer problems:

1. pythia_itemdata.tsv contained a header row, and HTCondor queue-from
   treated that header as a fifth job.

2. Two argument separators were missing in pythia_rehearsal.sub:

   $(source_stop)--output-root

   $(max_events)--executable

Observed consequence:
- dry-run materialized 5 jobs rather than 4
- ProcId 0 used literal column names as argument values
- some real-job arguments were concatenated

Physics/sample products were not affected.
No PYTHIA/HepMC3 job was submitted.

Corrective action:
- preserve the human-readable headered TSV
- generate a separate headerless HTCondor queue TSV
- rewrite submit arguments with explicit whitespace
- repeat dry-run and require exactly four jobs
