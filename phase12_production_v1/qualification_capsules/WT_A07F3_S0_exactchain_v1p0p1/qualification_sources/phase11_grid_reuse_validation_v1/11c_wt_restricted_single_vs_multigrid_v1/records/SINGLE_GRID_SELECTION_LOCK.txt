Selection rule:
S0 is selected because it is the first canonical seed identifier in the
predefined S0--S7 family and is being fixed before any Phase-11C WT

- cross section value,
- integration uncertainty,
- generation efficiency,
- excess-weight behavior,
- event distributions,
- or any later observable.

qualified A07/F3 family and are reserved as independent multi-grid references.

No post-generation substitution of S0, S1, or S2 is allowed on the basis of
a more favorable numerical or physics result.

Technical execution failures may be replayed only with the exact same grid
and exact same RNG seed.
