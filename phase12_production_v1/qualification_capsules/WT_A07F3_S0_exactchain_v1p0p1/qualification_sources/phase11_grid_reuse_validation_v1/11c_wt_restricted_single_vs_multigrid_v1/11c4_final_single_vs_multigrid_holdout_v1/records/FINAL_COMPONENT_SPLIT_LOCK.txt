PHASE-11C FINAL HOLDOUT COMPONENT-SPLIT LOCK
=============================================

Purpose:
Divide the frozen 64k SINGLE and 64k MULTI samples into four 32k
analysis-packaging components required by the qualified legacy validator.

This split is frozen before holdout generation.

SINGLE
------

SINGLE_A:
32 S0 shards

SINGLE_B:
32 S0 shards

Assignment:
odd-numbered S0 holdout shards -> SINGLE_A
even-numbered S0 holdout shards -> SINGLE_B

Legacy validator mapping:
SINGLE_A -> S0_C
SINGLE_B -> S0_D

Therefore:
S0_C = 32,000 events
S0_D = 32,000 events


MULTI
-----

Grid    MULTI_A    MULTI_B    Total
S1         5          5        10
S2         5          4         9
S3         4          5         9
S4         5          4         9
S5         4          5         9
S6         5          4         9
S7         4          5         9

Totals:
MULTI_A = 32 shards = 32,000 events
MULTI_B = 32 shards = 32,000 events

Legacy validator mapping:
MULTI_A -> S1_B
MULTI_B -> S2_B

Within each grid, assignment is deterministic by local shard index.

S1:
local shards 1-5  -> MULTI_A
local shards 6-10 -> MULTI_B

S2, S4, S6:
odd local shards  -> MULTI_A
even local shards -> MULTI_B

S3, S5, S7:
odd local shards  -> MULTI_B
even local shards -> MULTI_A

This split is analysis packaging only.

The primary MULTI sample is always the pooled union of all 64 qualified
S1-S7 holdout shards.

No split, grid membership, threshold, or sample composition may be changed
after physics distributions are inspected.
