Phase-9 precedent relevant to Phase-11C.5G
==========================================

The existing project documentation records prior pathological
WHIZARD unweighting behavior during the 365-GeV full-6f campaign.

Relevant documented observations include:

- monolithic/difficult event-generation jobs could encounter rare
  high-weight tails;

- a documented 500-event diagnostic produced only 103/500 raw
  events after approximately 56 minutes because of a rare
  high-weight spike;

- the Phase-9 workflow adopted smaller 500-event shards so that a
  pathological unweighting seed affected only one small shard rather
  than invalidating a full monolithic sample;

- Phase-9 operational documentation permitted pathological shards to
  be removed and retried.

Relevance to Phase 11
---------------------

This establishes that rare unweighting-tail behavior predates the
Phase-11 frozen-grid reuse tests.

However, the Phase-9 remove-and-retry policy cannot automatically be
adopted for the precision Phase-11 validation.

For Phase 11 we must distinguish:

1. an operationally retryable failed production shard;

2. evidence that a frozen integration/envelope is statistically
   insufficient;

3. selection bias introduced by repeatedly regenerating until a
   desired envelope diagnostic is obtained.

Therefore no new C.5 holdout replacement shards will be selected
until the WHIZARD excess-weight definition and generation-envelope
behavior have been audited.
