# Physics references

1. CMS Collaboration, “Measurement of the top quark polarization and ttbar spin
   correlations using dilepton final states in proton-proton collisions at
   sqrt(s)=13 TeV,” Phys. Rev. D 100, 072002 (2019), arXiv:1907.03729.

2. W. Bernreuther, D. Heisler, and Z.-G. Si, “A set of top quark spin correlation
   and polarization observables for the LHC: Standard Model predictions and new
   physics contributions,” JHEP 12 (2015) 026, arXiv:1508.05271.

3. F. Maltoni, C. Severi, S. Tentori, and E. Vryonidou, “Quantum tops at circular
   lepton colliders,” JHEP 09 (2024) 001, arXiv:2404.08049.

4. CMS Collaboration, “Observation of quantum entanglement in top quark pair
   production in proton-proton collisions at sqrt(s)=13 TeV,”
   arXiv:2406.03976.

The CMS methodology motivates complete angular-observable extraction,
covariance propagation, closure tests, and controls. The lepton-collider paper
sets the basis and density-matrix convention appropriate to the current
`e+e-` and `mu+mu-` samples.
